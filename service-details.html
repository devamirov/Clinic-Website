General Consultation

Personalized primary care visits that combine preventive screening with evidence-based treatment.

Our physicians take time to understand your history, current concerns, and lifestyle so every visit results in a practical care plan. We coordinate labs, imaging, and specialist referrals on your behalf and follow up to ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Whether you need an annual physical or support managing a chronic condition, we monitor progress and offer proactive guidance between visits to keep you moving toward your health goals.

What to Expect

  • Medication reconciliation and care coordination with specialists
  • Lifestyle coaching covering nutrition, fitness, sleep, and stress
