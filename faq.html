Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about booking visits, preparing for appointments, and getting follow-up support from the RMC team.

Still Have Questions?

Our patient relations team is available every day to help you with scheduling, insurance verification, medical records, and anything else you need.

How do I book an appointment at RMC?

Request a visit online through our appointment page, call us at +961 78 73 63 75, or message us on WhatsApp. Share your preferred date, the reason for the visit, and any insurance details so we can confirm quickly.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

We work with most national and regional insurance providers. When you contact us, our team will verify your coverage, explain any co-payments, and let you know if pre-authorization is needed.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring a photo ID, insurance card, current medication list, and any recent lab or imaging reports. Arrive 15 minutes early so you can complete consent and medical history forms.

How can I access test results or medical reports?

Your physician will review results with you during a follow-up call or visit. You can also request a copy through our medical records desk or by emailing contact.rmc@yahoo.com.
